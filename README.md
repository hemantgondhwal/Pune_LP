# The XL Academy - Pune Landing Page

High-converting, mobile-responsive landing page for **The XL Academy's Data Analytics & Data Science** training programs in Pune (Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Baner & Magarpatta).

## Features
- **Responsive Design**: Fluid layout optimized across mobile devices, tablets, and wide screens.
- **Dynamic Course Section**: Visual course cards for Data Analytics with Python, Data Science with ML, Business Analytics, and MIS & Reporting with custom course previews and ratings.
- **Urgency & Lead Conversion**: Multi-tier enquiry forms (Hero form, mid-page demo form, popups, and sticky mobile action bar).
- **Interactive Reviews & Placements**: Carousel sliders for placement stories, hiring company marquee, and alumni reviews with touch swipe support.
- **Lead Capture Integration**: Direct integration via `send_mail.php` with Google Sheets webhook and instant redirect to `thankyou.html`.
- **Pune Branch Locations**: Interactive branch cards with Google Maps links and phone/WhatsApp CTAs.
